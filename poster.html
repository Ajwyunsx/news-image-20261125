Trade and markets

Washington readies new tariffs on sixteen trading partners

A Section 301 finding on “excess industrial capacity” lands before Thanksgiving, and stocks slip as the AI trade cools.

The Trump administration was preparing to publish the results of a probe into alleged excess factory capacity across sixteen major trading partners, with new import duties expected to stack on top of tariffs already imposed this term. Officials signalled that the findings could come within days, and that the levies would be phased in on a rolling schedule rather than all at once.

China, India, Japan, South Korea, Mexico, Vietnam, Taiwan, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Switzerland, Norway, Singapore, Bangladesh, Cambodia and the EU are covered. Rates of 15 to 25 per cent have been mooted; Beijing dismissed the overcapacity charge but stopped short of threatening retaliation.
